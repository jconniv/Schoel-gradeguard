Schoel GradeGuard v8.4.8 Patch Notes

Purpose:
- Harden HP/LP protection.

Changes:
- Added HP/LP hard-lock routine.
- True HP, LP, HP_TC, and LP_TC callouts now force the exact callout elevation at the leader/tick/projected BC point.
- HP/LP hard-lock runs before station contour profile corrections and again after the field-elevation sanitizer.
- HP/LP points are never averaged, interpolated through, or overwritten by BC station contour interpolation.
- HP/LP points with no trusted callout elevation remain 0.000 and flagged for review.
- Added debug stats:
  - v8_4_8_hp_lp_hard_locked
  - v8_4_8_hp_lp_relocked_after_sanitize
  - v8_4_8_hp_lp_hold_rule

Survey rule:
- Normal BC points interpolate by station between contour controls.
- True HP/LP points are vertical break points and hold the callout elevation exactly.
