Schoel GradeGuard v8.4.2 Patch Notes

Purpose:
- Keep the v8.4.1 BC station interpolation fix.
- Protect true high points and low points so they are not averaged away.

Changes:
1. HP/LP plan callouts are treated as hard vertical controls.
2. Existing HP/LP elevations are never replaced by station interpolation.
3. If a point is identified as BC HP or BC LP but has no trustworthy held elevation, GradeGuard leaves it as 0.000 for review instead of averaging through it.
4. Fixed sanitizer wording so LOW POINT / LP callouts are not rejected because the word LOW appears in the source or issue text.
5. Added v8_4_2_hp_lp_hold_rule to the debug/report stats.

Survey rule:
- Interpolation is correct between contour controls along the BC station chain.
- HP/LP callouts are not smoothing points. They are break points and must hold when the PDF/CAD callout gives an elevation.
- If a HP/LP is marked but the elevation is not known, it must be reviewed manually rather than guessed.
