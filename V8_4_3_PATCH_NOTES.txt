Schoel GradeGuard v8.4.3 Patch Notes

Purpose:
- Remove the manual QA checklist requirement.
- Keep QA/QC automatic inside the program and reports.

Changes:
1. Replaced the visible manual QA checklist with an Automatic QA/QC information panel.
2. Generate no longer stops because checkboxes were not manually checked.
3. Civil PDF is still required for BC generation.
4. PDF callouts/details still control over CAD/DXF.
5. Catch curb remains +0.42 ft and spill curb remains +0.58 ft.
6. BC station interpolation from v8.4.1 remains active.
7. HP/LP protection from v8.4.2 remains active.
8. Unsupported points still export as 0.000 and are flagged in the review/source report.
9. Debug/report output now records v8.4.3 automatic QA/QC rule.

Important:
- The manual checklist was removed because the user wants GradeGuard to perform automatic QA/QC instead of requiring the drafter to check boxes.
