Schoel GradeGuard v8.4.7 Patch Notes

Main fix:
- GradeGuard now accounts for the distance along the selected BC station to the contour control.
- Short-gap contours projected to the selected BC are treated as direct BC contour controls.
- Direct BC contour controls do not receive curb height.
- Normal BC points can now be corrected from the selected-BC contour profile even when they already had a wrong nonzero elevation from a lower-priority FL/gutter or generic surface source.

Survey rule:
- Contour at BC = exact contour elevation.
- Point between BC contour controls = interpolate/prorate by station distance along BC.
- FL/gutter/EP contour = add verified curb height only when it truly comes from the selected FL/gutter/EP.
- HP/LP points remain protected and are not averaged through.

Why this matters:
- A contour may stop short of the curb line in CAD but clearly controls the curb location on the PDF/DXF.
- The program now projects that contour to the selected BC, records its station, then measures every generated BC point by station distance to that contour control.
