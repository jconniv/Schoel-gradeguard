Schoel GradeGuard v8.4.5

Fixes curb height default.

Previous v8.4 builds defaulted to catch curb +0.42. That was wrong for standard curb work.

v8.4.5 behavior:
- Standard curb is the default and uses +0.50 ft.
- Catch curb +0.42 is used only when selected based on the certified PDF detail.
- Spill curb +0.58 is used only when selected based on the certified PDF detail.
- Custom height is used only when it comes from the certified PDF detail.
- BC station interpolation remains active.
- HP/LP protection remains active.
- PDF to DXF callout endpoint anchoring remains active.
- Civil PDF remains required.

This is meant to prevent GradeGuard from adding +0.42 to standard curb projects that only call out normal 18 inch curb and gutter.
