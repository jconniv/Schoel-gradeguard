Schoel GradeGuard v8.5.7 Patch Notes

Fix:
Point 6006 type jump near a low point cluster.

Problem:
A normal BC point that was nearly on top of a BC LP could be pulled to an isolated endpoint extension contour beyond the curb end. This created a false vertical cliff between adjacent points.

Rule:
HP and LP controls are vertical break locations. Normal BC points within 1.25 ft of a trusted HP or LP hold that HP or LP elevation unless a direct PDF/manual callout overrides it.

Still locked:
Contour hits actual selected BC: hold exact contour elevation.
Contour hits selected EP, FL, or gutter: add curb height.
Standard curb height: +0.50 ft unless the PDF detail says otherwise.
Normal points between controls: prorate by station along the connected BC run.
HP and LP: always hold and are never averaged through.

Added:
AutoLISP importer for CAD named GradeGuard_Import_BC_Points.lsp.
