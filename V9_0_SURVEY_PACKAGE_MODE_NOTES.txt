Schoel GradeGuard v9.0 Survey Package Mode

v9.0.3 BOC ambiguity safety patch (2026-08-28):
- BOC may mean BACK OF CURB or BOTTOM OF CURB depending on the engineer.
- Bare BOC HP/LP callouts are review-only and never automatically hard-held or converted.
- Confirm the engineer's plan legend/convention, then use a manual tick anchor with the verified meaning.

v9.0.2 HP/LP release-safety patch (2026-08-28):
- Compound HP/LP curb callouts are parsed once at their strongest meaning.
- HP EL./ELEV and LP EL./ELEV forms are recognized.
- Automatic PDF HP/LP hard holds require a real leader/tick endpoint and controlled PDF-to-CAD alignment.
- Approximate or text-center PDF detections require office review and a manual tick anchor.

v9.0.1 performance patch (2026-08-28):
- BC generation now runs in a guarded background worker so the window remains responsive on older computers.
- The Generate button shows an in-progress state and blocks accidental duplicate jobs.
- Dense curb point de-duplication now avoids slow all-to-all comparisons.
- Survey calculations, outputs, coordinates, elevations, and QA rules are unchanged.

This build keeps the v8.5.7 curb logic and adds survey-office package outputs.

Locked rules:
1. PDF controls.
2. DXF gives geometry.
3. Proposed contours build the grading surface.
4. Contour on actual selected BC holds exact elevation.
5. Contour on selected EP, FL, or gutter adds curb height.
6. Standard curb height is +0.50 ft unless the PDF detail says otherwise.
7. Normal BC points between controls prorate by station along the connected BC run.
8. HP and LP always hold. They are never averaged through.
9. Bad jumps and unsupported elevations are flagged for office QA.
10. No separate Crew View Report is created.
