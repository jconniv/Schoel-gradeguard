Schoel GradeGuard v8.5.1 Surface Mode

This build changes curb grading from scattered point logic to surface-first logic.

Rules added:
1. Build a temporary proposed grading surface from 3D DXF contours when no LandXML surface is supplied.
2. Sample normal BC points from that contour surface.
3. Direct contour at BC holds exact contour elevation with no curb height added.
4. PDF/manual TC/BC callouts hold first.
5. HP and LP callouts remain hard vertical controls and are never averaged through.
6. BC station interpolation remains as QA/fallback after the surface.
7. Storm/low areas are respected through the contour surface and water-flow review; storm inverts are not used as BC unless a top/grate/curb callout verifies them.

This specifically addresses points like 6044, 6004, 6016, and 6017 where the previous builds were pulling from station/profile logic instead of the proposed grading surface.
