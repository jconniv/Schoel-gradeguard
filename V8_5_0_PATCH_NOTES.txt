Schoel GradeGuard v8.5.0 patch notes

Critical grading fixes:
- Direct contour at BC now holds directly and never receives +0.50 curb height.
- Fixes points such as 6044 where the 610 contour is at the generated BC point.
- Adds BC_DIRECT_CONTOUR_HOLD_V850 as a trusted source.
- Adds a second contour-profile proration pass after direct contour holds.
- Proration uses BC station controls and is not allowed to overwrite PDF/manual/HP/LP holds.
- Keeps standard curb +0.50 only for true selected FL/gutter/EP controls.
- HP and LP remain hard vertical controls.

Storm/drainage workflow note:
- Storm linework should be used to identify drainage paths and inlet/low-point review areas.
- Storm invert elevations are not used as BC elevations unless the user adds a separate verified top/grate/curb elevation callout.
