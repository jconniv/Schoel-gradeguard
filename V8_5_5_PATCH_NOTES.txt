Schoel GradeGuard v8.5.5 Patch Notes

Fixed crash:
- AttributeError: CurbSegment object has no attribute source
- Cause: v8.5.4 connected curb-run ordering reversed CurbSegment objects using a fourth source argument.
- CurbSegment only has a, b, and layer.
- Reversed segments now preserve a, b, and layer only.

Preserved grading logic:
- BC contour hit holds exact contour elevation.
- EP/FL/gutter contour hit adds standard +0.50 ft unless PDF detail says otherwise.
- Normal BC points prorate/average by station between contour controls.
- HP and LP points remain protected and are not averaged through.
