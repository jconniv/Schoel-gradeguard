SCHOEL GRADEGUARD 9.6.0 - THREE STEPS
Local guided workflow pilot, 2026-10-29

1. OPEN PLANS
   Choose the plan PDF. If you have a matching DXF and/or LandXML, check the
   extra-files option and choose them. Continue opens the appropriate workflow.
2. CALCULATE
   With CAD: choose curb, sidewalk and/or grade grid and calculate together.
   Review the displayed spacing, curb rise and surface interpretation first.
   With PDF only: follow Sheet & scale, Paths & grades, Calculate & review.
   Next buttons explain what is needed. Known-distance mode hides survey
   coordinate fields. Grade meanings use plain words from the plan legend.
   Save setup to reuse a checked sheet without repeating all the picks.
3. REVIEW RESULTS
   After calculating, choose Inspect points & profiles. From the home screen,
   Review results opens the current run, or lets you choose a saved
   Calculation-Review.json. Packages created before 9.6 need recalculation
   to include this new review file.

ADVANCED SETTINGS
Use Advanced settings to access Files & setup and Tool settings. Individual
curb, sidewalk, structure and grid tools remain available there. New/Open/Save
job remain at the top. PDF setup files and main job files remain separate.
LandXML without a DXF does not provide path geometry: use the guided PDF
workflow for curb/sidewalk paths, or the existing advanced surface/grid tool.

POINT REVIEW
Click a point on the plan, point list or profile. Selection stays linked.
Choose a path to see elevation versus distance along it. Arrows point toward
the lower grade; signed slope is 100 times rise divided by station distance.
The vertical scale is exaggerated and is labeled accordingly.
Green means accepted by the existing export checks, gold means calculated
for review/withheld, red means missing. Missing elevations stay blank and
break the profile; disconnected runs are not joined. CAD branches shared by
several runs are flagged instead of assigning an arbitrary profile station.
Grid points and curb offsets do not acquire a fabricated path profile.

PDF interpolation records the actual bracket stations and elevations, held
HP/LP controls, and the entered curb conversion. Top/back grades hold directly;
flowline grades add the verified local rise once. Triangle-based surface
evidence shows vertices, barycentric weights and one subgrade deduction.
Other CAD methods retain original engine source/QA text where intermediate
operands are unavailable. The viewer never guesses those missing operands.

View PDF evidence opens the actual printed callout or the manually located
tick area. Source fingerprints must match; changed/missing plans disable
the PDF background/evidence and flag results as historical. Inspection does
not edit source plans, recalculate grades, change acceptance or approve points.

COLORS AND OPENING
The main workspace, calculation windows and review screens use the opening
screen's navy, muted green and gold. Plan paper remains white. Replay opening
still shows the animated dozer. Small home windows can scroll to every action.

LIMITS
PDF-only calculations still need a checked scale, units, reference, selected
paths and verified grade meanings/locations. They are office-review outputs,
not approved field files. Spot-only grids do not supply design breaklines.
Software tests are not field-accuracy validation. See AUTO-CALCULATE-9.5.0.txt
for the underlying calculation workflow and CALCULATION-FIXES-9.3.1.txt for
the existing export/calculation safeguards.
