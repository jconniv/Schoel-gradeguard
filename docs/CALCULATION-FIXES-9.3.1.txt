Changes tied to the audit
------------------------
1. Held controls: manual TC 610.000 stays 610.000 through a complete generation
   run. Source provenance and approval persist. Legacy correction passes no
   longer repeatedly rewrite final grades. Explicit HP/LP and sidewalk holds
   are protected. Automatically inferred extrema are QA notes, not renamed stakes.

2. Curb component: actual BC contour crossings hold their elevation. Selected
   FL/gutter crossings add the entered curb height. The audited midpoint is
   now 601.500, including the 0.500-foot addition. A near/short-gap contour
   cannot override the FL conversion or become an approved station anchor.

3. Connected runs: station interpolation uses the local continuous chain.
   Separate runs cannot share station controls. Branched chains and drafted
   gaps are not treated as one continuous station profile. Conflicting grades
   at the same controlling station are withheld.

4. Job state: all generation tools reset cached geometry and overrides. The
   audited fresh subgrade result is 599.500 even after a different curb job.
   Saved geometry selections and manual controls are checked against the
   DXF path and SHA-256. Changed drawings and old unbound selections require
   review and re-saving. Sidewalk/structure settings now save and load with jobs.

5. Field export: curb, sidewalk, grid, structures and offsets share an approval
   gate. A numeric elevation alone cannot bypass it. Unknown, conflicting,
   review-only, nonfinite or zero results are omitted from field CSVs. Review
   reports retain rejected calculations in an explicitly review-only column.
   Offset approval comes from its parent BC point; offsets keep the BC elevation.

6. LandXML: reads the documented Northing/Easting/Elevation order, selects one
   named surface, scopes point IDs to that surface, checks face references and
   declared units, excludes hidden faces, and rejects malformed files visibly.
   Multiple surfaces require selection. Feature-line-only XML is rejected;
   export the intended TIN points and faces instead of relying on curve chords.

7. Source precedence: source selection now controls the runner. Preferred
   LandXML cannot be replaced by a temporary contour TIN. Explicit DXF-surface
   mode prioritizes a supported surface edge. Contours-only mode needs actual
   connected contour controls. Surface BC versus FL meaning must be explicit
   where it cannot be established from the source.

8. Interpolation: geometric bracketing is required; same-side contours cannot
   masquerade as a bracket. Conflicting duplicate XY grades reject temporary
   TIN creation. Outside-surface and unconstrained temporary-TIN estimates stay
   review-only. A nearby grade is not automatically a field grade.

9. PDF alignment: corrected the reflected similarity-transform signs and
   degeneracy handling. Explicit manual PDF controls with inadequate alignment
   are rejected. Automatic text-center/extents alignment and unverified sheet
   registration are review-only even when fit residual happens to be small.

10. Labels: ambiguous BOC and unsupported HP/LP variants cannot supply an
    approved grade. Held manual provenance survives label lookup. Conflicting
    colocated controls are reported. Ordinary nearby text requires review of
    its actual physical control location.

11. Structures: OUT elevations belong to the matching structure ID. IN-only
    and conflicting OUT records are withheld. Closed polygon centers use area
    centroid instead of weighting the closing vertex twice. The audited square
    center is (2,2). PDF text windows no longer include the preceding structure.

12. Curves: corrected major positive/negative bulges, exact bulge midpoints,
    full ellipse parameters, ARC WCS coordinates and 2D polyline OCS conversion.
    Added negative-extrusion, 3D-polyline and rotated/scaled-block examples.

13. Sidewalk: local conflicts are flagged instead of replacing held design
    grades with a neighbor median. Automatic cross-slope changes require review;
    explicit selected adjustments remain width * percent / 100.

14. Numeric handling: removed the audited project-specific grade limits,
    retains a DXF zero endpoint instead of substituting the other endpoint,
    excludes CAD thickness as an elevation, and rejects invalid spacing and
    nonfinite/negative calculation settings. Zero grades remain withheld by
    policy and need office handling, even on a legitimate zero-datum site.

15. Diagnostics: flow/conflict pairs follow station order within continuous
    unbranched runs and do not bridge withheld intermediate grades. These are
    grade diagnostics; they are not a hydraulic/drainage design calculation.

Additional safeguards
---------------------
- DXF must declare US survey feet (code 21), or a unitless drawing must be
  explicitly confirmed as US survey feet in Project files. Other unit systems
  are rejected. No silent coordinate/unit/datum transformation is performed.
- Reruns preserve existing products in the selected output folder and create a
  new Run-date-time subfolder when earlier GradeGuard products are present.
- Both AutoLISP import helpers accept quoted CSV fields, validate numeric
  coordinates/elevations and skip RED/unknown/blank/zero rows. The old 3D command
  aliases import points only; they no longer connect every CSV row by order.
- The guided interface and animated dozer opening are retained.

Using this build
----------------
1. Open Project files and choose the local DXF, PDF, output directory and
   optional LandXML. Confirm the coordinate references and design authority.
2. Select the intended LandXML surface name. Multiple surfaces cannot be merged
   implicitly. Only check unitless-DXF confirmation after checking actual units.
3. Re-pick BC/FL/sidewalk objects when an old selection lacks drawing validation
   or the DXF has changed. Review and re-save manual controls for that drawing.
4. Choose the tool, source preference and surface meaning. Confirm local curb
   details, especially ramps/transitions; a preset is user input, not proof that
   the software verified the PDF or that one height applies everywhere.
5. Generate, review all warning and DO_NOT_STAKE reports, and compare independent
   project controls/check values before accepting field CSVs.

Intentional limits
------------------
No automated verification of signed plans, design authority, datum/geoid,
benchmark/control residuals or survey accuracy is provided. Grid extents remain
a buffered rectangle, not an irregular-site boundary clip. Users must verify
layer meaning, physical label ownership, selected curb/FL correspondence, local
curb detail applicability and the intended design surface. Unsupported surface
formats and uncertain calculations are rejected or withheld, not guessed.
