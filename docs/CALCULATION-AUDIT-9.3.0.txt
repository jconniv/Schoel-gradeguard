SCHOEL GRADEGUARD 9.3.0 - CALCULATION AND WORKFLOW AUDIT
Audit date: October 28, 2026
Status: UNRESOLVED CALCULATION / DATA-INTEGRITY FINDINGS

Conclusion
----------
Do not treat the current preview as validated for unattended field staking.
The underlying elementary math works in many tested examples, but source
selection, geometry interpretation, topology, control preservation, and export
gating have reproducible failures. These can change actual field coordinates or
elevations; they are not only wording or interface issues.

The original 19 tests pass. A new, deliberately adversarial audit contains 78
checks: 40 pass, 38 fail, zero execution errors. Several checks reproduce the
same underlying defect at different levels. These counts are not 38 distinct
bugs and are not an estimate of the percentage of real jobs that are correct.

This is an audit, not a corrected release. Application code, calculations,
deployed executable, and shortcuts remain unchanged at 9.3.0. Added files are
the reproducible audit runner and this report, with a validation changelog entry.
No real project, original survey record, or field data was modified.

How the program actually works
-----------------------------
GradeGuard is a local Python/Tk application. schoel_gradeguard.py contains the
calculation engine, import/export functions, job state, and selection windows.
gradeguard_ui.py presents four tools. gradeguard_splash.py is presentation only.
It prepares design-derived stake coordinates from engineer-supplied sources;
it does not perform a survey adjustment, certify the design, model hydraulics,
or calculate earthwork volumes.

1. DXF supplies XY geometry. Internal XY means Easting, Northing. LINEs are
   sampled at requested spacing. Curves normally get PC, midpoint, and PT;
   denser tessellation supports intersections and picking. True BC and FL may
   be selected by entity handle, otherwise layer-name rules select objects.
   Text labels, contour Z, surface edges, and leader-like lines are extracted.

2. Elevation evidence comes from CAD text, manually entered PDF tick anchors,
   automatically matched PDF callouts, LandXML faces/feature lines, and DXF
   contours/surface edges. Automatic PDF matching uses coordinate-label text
   centers, or falls back to matching sheet/CAD extents. The ordinary GUI keeps
   general automatic PDF spots disabled, while separately screening automatic
   HP/LP labels using an alignment RMSE and vector-leader gate.

3. Curb generation first creates spacing/curve/control/intersection points.
   assign_elevations evaluates held labels, exact overrides, selected BC/FL
   contour controls, local contour interpolation, LandXML, contour TIN, and
   nearby fallback sources. Actual implementation does NOT consistently match
   the documented claim that supplied LandXML is the preferred non-PDF source.

4. The curb result then passes through HP/LP hard holds, temporary contour
   surface corrections, flow tagging, station-profile corrections, nearby
   contour holds, profile rebuilding, a second surface correction, missing-grade
   filling, another flow pass, HP/LP clustering, the sanitizer, and another
   hard-hold pass. Several stages mutate the same elevation and append text to
   its provenance. A correct initial value can be lost later.

5. Curb export writes headerless P,N,E,Z,D values to three decimals, a source
   CSV, confidence summary, conflict report, DO_NOT_STAKE list, HTML report,
   water-flow PDF, and manifest. Contractor offsets move XY by the requested
   distance and KEEP THE BC ELEVATION; they do not calculate ground elevation
   at the offset location. Separate CSV/DXF outputs show the offset relationship.

6. The other tools share some helpers but differ materially:
   - Sidewalk: selected edge geometry; labels/LandXML/perpendicular contours/
     contour TIN/fallback; optional width*slope adjustment; local median
     replacement and optional cross-slope clamping; direct CSV export.
   - Grade grid: buffered rectangular extents, not a clipped closed boundary;
     grade assignment using the curb helper; Z_subgrade = Z_finished - thickness;
     direct CSV export. The grid does not build the contour TIN used by curb.
   - Storm/sanitary: detect structure centers, gather nearby invert text and PDF
     label windows, choose the lowest explicit OUT (or lowest invert fallback),
     append manually picked structures, then directly export.

The last three tools do not call the curb sanitizer before write_csv.

Arithmetic verified in the stated examples
-----------------------------------------
- Euclidean distances, clamped segment projection, ordinary intersections,
  straight 25-foot station spacing, ordinary quarter-circle controls, and
  positive/negative minor bulges agree with independent known answers.
- Barycentric triangle interpolation agrees with Z=600+0.1X+0.2Y, including
  200 deterministic interior points at large grid coordinates. A basic
  contour TIN reproduces a plane. Feature-line Z interpolates linearly.
- A clean 600-to-602 BC profile over 40 feet gives 601 at station 20, including
  a full DXF/PDF generation and CSV check.
- Explicit FL + 0.50, direct TC holds, the dedicated FL-touch helper,
  and a trusted 612.345 HP hold pass their isolated examples.
- Width*slope/100, explicit sidewalk add/subtract, direct mode, the numerical
  cross-slope clamp, ray distance, and a ten-foot detected sidewalk width pass.
- Three-foot offsets and reversed offsets move to the expected side and keep Z.
- Two-point and non-reflected multi-point similarity transforms pass.
- A flat 600-foot surface minus 0.50 outputs 599.50 subgrade in a fresh run.
- PNEZD ordering and three-decimal output rounding pass. None and zero are
  omitted. Ordinary LOW_CONF curb fallback is withheld. A 0.02-foot rise over
  ten feet is correctly reported as 0.20 percent.
- Six-inch detail conversion and the isolated conflicting-height helper pass.

Reproducible findings requiring correction
-----------------------------------------
Source locations below refer to unchanged schoel_gradeguard.py in the active
C:\SchoelTools\SchoelGradeGuard checkout. Test IDs refer to calculation_audit.py.

1. BLOCKER: Held controls can be overwritten.
   R05: full generation changed a manual TC 610.000 at station 20 to 601.000.
   C11: source assignment turns a manual anchor with raw text 'TC 650.00' into
   'CAD spot'; later string-based hold protection fails and TIN replaces it
   with 605.000. Source provenance must remain structured and immutable.
   Locations: nearest_label_elevation:2212, apply_bc_station_contour_profile_to_
   normal_points:3980, apply_contour_surface_mode_v851:4388, run_generation:5186.

2. BLOCKER: Curb height can be lost at selected flowline controls.
   C06/R06: an actual FL crossing three feet from BC is also treated as a
   'short-gap BC' contour. The short-gap candidate outranks the actual FL hit.
   Full generation returned 601.000 at the midpoint instead of 601.500.
   C12 also demonstrates an intermediate exact BC override being overwritten
   by FL at the same coordinate. R07 shows that later corrections DO repair
   the tested case where the same contours cross both BC and FL. Do not
   generalize C12 alone as an uncorrected final CSV error in every such case.
   Locations: add_contour_crossing_bc_points:1489,
   curb_chain_contour_elevation:3293, run_generation:5186.

3. BLOCKER: Disconnected curb runs share station controls.
   C09: a missing grade 990 feet away inherited 600.000 because global station
   accumulation omits the inter-run gap. C14: profile rebuilding mixed local
   station numbers from separate components and changed 650.000 to 601.000.
   Component identity is missing in the correction-stage anchor matching.
   Locations: _bc_chain_station_for_point:3041,
   fill_missing_bc_elevations_by_station_interpolation:3859,
   rebuild_bc_profile_from_contours_v850:4268.

4. BLOCKER: Earlier curb-job state can contaminate a grade-grid run.
   R08: a previous exact contour override of 620 at the next grid coordinate
   produced subgrade 619.500 from a newly supplied flat 600 surface; expected
   599.500. DXF loading resets curb geometry but not the override dictionary
   or all flowline state. Only the curb runner explicitly resets overrides.
   Locations: globals:87-103, load_dxf_geometry_and_elevations:1182,
   assign_elevations:3467, run_grade_grid_generation:6005.

5. BLOCKER: Field export and QA status disagree.
   E04: a point explicitly marked NOT BRACKETED stayed numeric after the curb
   sanitizer because TIN_CONTOUR appeared in its history. Its QA status was RED,
   yet write_csv still accepted it. C13: detecting a possible HP promotes a
   LOW_CONF estimate into an accepted field elevation. E03: positive infinity
   is accepted by stake_ready_points. That function only tests nonzero Z;
   it does not establish source approval, finite XY/Z, or final QA status.
   R01/R03/P02: sidewalk, grid, and FL OUT runs exported review-only values
   without applying the curb sanitizer. Export approval needs a common gate.
   Locations: tag_high_low_points:1650, sanitize_field_elevations:4509,
   stake_ready_points:4644, gg_v9_status_for_point:4697;
   runners:5698,5931,6005.

6. BLOCKER: LandXML axes, surfaces, and reference metadata are not reliable.
   I01: NEZ '1000 2000 600' becomes XY=(1000,2000), expected (2000,1000).
   I02 passes only because that coordinate magnitude matches the heuristic.
   I03: two surfaces reusing IDs 1/2/3 lose the first surface's vertices; both
   faces use the last surface's points (700 instead of separate 600 and 700).
   I04: declared meter data is accepted without conversion or rejection in
   the stated foot workflow. The importer does not select a named surface,
   enforce CRS/datum/vertical reference, or validate units against DXF.
   LandXML point order is NEZ; see ICSM LandXML Mapping, section 3.42:
   https://www.anzlic.gov.au/sites/default/files/files/ePlan-Protocol-LandXML-Mapping-v2.1.4.pdf
   Locations: _parse_landxml_xyz:817, load_landxml_surface:843.

7. HIGH: Selected LandXML can silently disappear or lose precedence.
   I05: corrupt selected XML returns None with no propagated error, enabling
   fallback. C10: later TIN correction replaces an existing LandXML 650.000
   with 605.000. The UI source_mode is saved and controls a missing-file prompt
   but is not passed as a source-selection policy to the generation engine.
   Locations: assign_elevations:3467, apply_contour_surface_mode_v851:4388,
   safe_load_landxml_surface:6081, GradeGuardApp.generate:7537.

8. HIGH: Contour interpolation can invent unsupported controls.
   S09: two contours both east of the point are treated as a bracket, returning
   600.666667 instead of an unresolved/review result. S07: coincident XY with
   600 and 640 Z is averaged to a trusted 620. S03: a point one foot outside a
   1000-foot triangle receives SURFACE_HIGH due to a dimensionless tolerance.
   Static review also found unrestricted Delaunay triangulation, no breakline
   constraints/hole boundaries, and no maximum triangle-edge check. These allow
   bridging gaps; the audit has not quantified errors on an actual project TIN.
   SciPy documents the triangulation and barycentric interface used here:
   https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.spatial.Delaunay.html
   Locations: landxml_elevation_at:945, build_contour_tin_surface:2655,
   local_contour_prorated_elevation:2895.

9. HIGH: PDF alignment has a reflected-transform sign error.
   T03: four exact controls for E=100+2X,N=200-2Y yield RMSE 14.142136 feet,
   rather than zero. The reflected least-squares row uses [y,-x] where the
   declared coefficient model needs [-y,x]. T04 also accepts coincident
   controls without an underdetermined-model warning. Automatic PDF extraction
   uses a separate affine routine, so T03 is specifically the saved/manual
   alignment path, not proof that every automatic overlay has this error.
   Static review: text centers are used as coordinate controls; the layout
   transform is reused on the grading page without verifying page registration.
   A small fit residual alone does not establish the correct physical tick.
   Locations: affine_from_layout_coordinates:1831,
   extract_pdf_spot_labels:2133, affine_transform_from_points:5047.

10. HIGH: Ambiguous BOC still supplies grades.
    C03: bare BOC 612.34 parses as BC. C04: HP_BOC_AMBIG falls through generic
    nearest-label lookup and supplies 612.34. This conflicts with the preflight
    statement that BOC is review-only. Parser detection and downstream approval
    need to enforce the same rule.
    Locations: parse_grade_text:132, nearest_label_elevation:2212.

11. HIGH: Structure identity and center calculations can be wrong.
    P03: DI-1 OUT 610 becomes 600 when nearby DI-2 has OUT 600; the runner takes
    the minimum of nearby values without excluding other structure identities.
    P02: an IN-only invert is exported as FL OUT with a review note.
    P04: a closed 4x4 square centers at (1.6,1.6), expected (2,2); its closing
    vertex is counted twice. Vertex averaging also is not a general centroid.
    PDF label windows can contain multiple neighboring structures (static review).
    P02/P03 mock candidate detection to isolate association; P04 uses a real
    synthetic DXF through the detector.
    Locations: load_dxf_structures_and_text:5564,
    extract_pdf_flout_text:5640, run_flout_generation:5698.

12. HIGH: Several supported curve geometries are misread.
    G08/G09: bulges +/-2 produce up to 7.50 feet radial disagreement on a
    ten-foot chord. The center-side formula fails for arcs exceeding 180 degrees.
    G10: full ellipse samples only 0..1 radians; minimum X is +5.403 instead
    of -10. G11: a negative-extrusion ARC starts at +10 instead of WCS -10.
    Independent ezdxf reference and coordinate-system documentation:
    https://ezdxf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/math/core.html#bulge-related-functions
    https://ezdxf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/dxfentities/ellipse.html
    https://ezdxf.readthedocs.io/en/stable/dxfentities/arc.html
    Locations: arc_points_from_entity:190, bulge_segment_points:240,
    picker_entity_points:338. Other OCS/polyline/block combinations still need
    dedicated geometry fixtures before being claimed correct.

13. HIGH: Sidewalk cleanup can change design rather than flag a conflict.
    W04: an explicit held 602 spot is replaced with neighboring median 600.
    Auto cross-slope mode also lowers the high side to its entered limit;
    the arithmetic passes W05, but a numerical clamp is not design approval.
    Direct/manual/LandXML holds need protection and reviewable proposed changes.
    Locations: enforce_sidewalk_cross_slope_pairs:2563,
    enforce_sidewalk_local_reasonableness:5891.

14. MEDIUM: Hard-coded elevation ranges and fallback substitutions remain.
    S10/S11: valid 600/610 contours and a 50/52-foot site are rejected by local
    interpolation. C08: HP hard-lock rejects 50 feet. Different functions use
    conflicting 100..2000, +/-10000, and up-to-100000 limits. I06: a DXF line
    from 600 to zero becomes a flat 600 line instead of retaining or rejecting
    the zero endpoint. entity_z also considers CAD thickness as possible Z.
    These are method-specific failures; another fallback may sometimes mask them.
    Locations: entity_z:710, DXF importer:1182,
    local_contour_prorated_elevation:2895, hard_lock_hp_lp_callout_elevations:3801.

15. MEDIUM: Flow diagnostics follow output order, not verified connectivity.
    F02: points on separate parallel runs are reported as one flat segment.
    Water-flow arrows and conflict checks also pair consecutive output points;
    they can join unrelated runs or skip missing intermediate points. The
    conflict threshold is a fixed elevation jump, not a project-approved grade
    threshold. These reports are diagnostics, not drainage validation.
    Locations: add_flow_warnings:3602,
    write_surface_conflict_report_csv:4758, write_water_flow_pdf:4851.

Additional static-review concerns, not counted as independently reproduced bugs
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Surface bounds, hidden/excluded faces, multiple models, LandXML curve geometry,
  and discontinuities are not represented robustly. Feature curves become chords.
- Curb presets override PDF-height verification and mark the user selection as
  verified; the program cannot verify the PDF is signed/certified or the chosen
  height applies to every local ramp, transition, catch, or spill section.
- Ordinary spot lookup uses a search radius, label kind, and source string rather
  than explicit ownership of a specific tick/curb component. CAD HP/LP projection
  can accept a text location up to 140 feet from the curb without a valid leader.
- Grid limits include a buffer around rectangular extents, including outside
  an actual irregular site boundary. Existing vs proposed contour layer selection
  must be explicit; a layer containing CONT is not evidence of design authority.
- Persistent selected-handle files store their CAD path, but loader selection is
  based on handles alone. Wrong-job selections need identity checks.
- Zero/negative/nonfinite settings and range limits are not consistently validated
  before calculation. Zero spacing can fail; negative spacing can omit stations.
- AutoLISP importers split CSV naively, convert Z through atof without checking
  blanks, and 3D commands connect all imported points by file order. Source review
  only: no AutoCAD/Civil 3D session was used to validate import behavior. Blank
  withheld elevations must not be allowed to become a believable plotted grade.
- Generated filenames are fixed; reruns can overwrite earlier review products.
- No project control/benchmark residual, US-survey-foot vs international-foot
  validation, horizontal/vertical datum transformation, or geoid check is present.
  The absence of a transformation is not evidence that input references agree.

Recommended correction sequence
-------------------------------
1. Implement a shared finite-coordinate and approved-source export gate for all
   four tools. A blocking status must prevent field export. Preserve excluded
   values only in explicit review reports. Prevent text-history tokens from
   promoting untrusted values or inferred HP/LP candidates.
2. Preserve typed source identity and immutable held controls. Reset all job
   state. Establish one documented precedence policy instead of successive
   unrestricted elevation-replacement passes. Wire the source selector to it.
3. Correct LandXML NEZ parsing and per-surface IDs; require surface selection and
   explicit units/references; fail visibly for unusable selected sources.
4. Fix connected-run stationing, exact BC/FL precedence, geometric imports,
   reflected alignment, and structure association/centers. Use focused regression
   cases rather than smoothing to hide disagreements.
5. Replace unsafe contour fallbacks with explicit review; preserve boundaries,
   breaklines and conflicts; make tolerances project-approved and traceable.
6. Rerun this audit plus nearby regressions, build a separately versioned preview,
   and compare real approved CAD/LandXML/PDF jobs with independent office checks.
   Include BC and FL controls, curves, HP/LP, ramps, separate runs, sidewalk edges,
   structure IN/OUT schedules, and subgrade points. Record residuals and sign-off.
